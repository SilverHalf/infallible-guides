# Mount Balrior

Mount Balrior is an extremely long wing that consists mainly of boss encounters. All three encounters in this wing are challenging, culminating with the fearsome Ura Legendary mode. Fast clears require good damage and clean execution of all three bosses. The Infallible timer itself is not too harrying, with most of the difficulty coming from the high risk of <img class='inline invuln'> [Downstates].

#### Main Points
{: .no_toc}
- [Decima] is played with a standard strategy.
- [Greer] is usually played without a tank.
- [Ura] can be played with either a [Solo CC] or a [4-man] composition.
- Portals are used to optimize transitions from pre-events into the various bosses.

---

## Composition

Most groups will run two <img class='inline mesmer'> [Mesmers] as they provide great utility and <img class='inline portal'> [Portals] for various transitions. These are usually <img class='inline troubadour'> [Troubadours], since they provide the best healing and defensive utility. At least one <img class='inline thief'> [Thief] is almost always present, since it can provide unparalleled CC.

The rest of the composition is mostly determined by your strategy on [Ura]:
- [Solo CC] compositions are much more flexible but require an experienced Solo CC player.
- [4-man] compositions are rigid but make ranged gameplay easier overall.

Prioritize consistency and safety over raw damage: redundant defensive utility, [Trailblazer] builds, and comfort picks wherever possible will all improve your experience progressing this wing. 

---

#### Decima
{: .no_toc}
- Bring reliable sources of <img class='inline stability'> [Stability] or <img class='inline aegis'> [Aegis] for [Seismic Crash], with <img class='inline aegis'> [Aegis] being better than <img class='inline stability'> [Stability].
- <img class='inline portal'> <img class='inline shadow-portal'> Portals are extremely useful for the split phases.
- Bring large amounts of CC for the <img class='inline defiance'> [Defiance Bar].
- <img class='inline troubadour'> [Troubadour] can be played with a celestial build and provides a large amount of useful utility.

#### Greer
{: .no_toc}
- <img class='inline condition'> Condition compositions are preferred, since most DPS can then run full [Trailblazer] stats with little downside.
- The bubble, if running with one, is often a heal <img class='inline vindicator'> [Vindicator] or <img class='inline luminary'> [Luminary].
- <img class='inline troubadour'> [Troubadour] can completely negate [Eruption of Rot] with <img class='inline august-queen'> [Tale of the August Queen].
- Classes with good cleave can make the split phases and Protoling phase much easier.
- Bring a large amount of projectile reflection.

#### Ura
{: .no_toc}
- Read the [Legendary Ura Composition Guide](https://silverhalf.github.io/mount-balrior/ura-lcm/strategy.html#composition).
