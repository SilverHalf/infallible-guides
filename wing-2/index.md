Salvation Pass is overall one of the easier wings to clear, and is often chosen by groups as the first wing they attempt on the road to the achievement.

The wing can become a struggle if groups lack DPS: speedrun strats for [Slothasor] involve eating a single <img class='inline shroom'> [Poison Mushroom], and longer [Matthias] pulls result in overall more dangerous mechanics to be played.

---

## Composition

Groups running Salvation Pass will usually run at least two <img class='inline mesmer'> [Mesmers] and one <img class='inline thief'> [Thief]. These can usually cover all encounter-specific utility, including:

- 10-man <img class='inline stealth'> [Stealth] for starting Slothasor.
- <img class='inline stability'> [Stability] for Slothasor and Matthias.
- <img class='inline pull'> [Pull] for Slothasor and Bandit Camp.
- <img class='inline invuln'> [Invulnerability] for Slothasor.
- <img class='inline portal'> [Portals] for the transition between Bandit Camp and Matthias.
- <img class='inline feedback'> [Projectile Reflection] for Matthias.

<img class='inline troubadour'> [Troubadour] is a common pick due to its strong solo-healing capabilities, with the other mesmer is being a support DPS <img class='inline chrono'> [Chronomancer] or DPS <img class='inline virtuoso'> [Virtuoso]. For the <img class='inline thief'> [Thief], <img class='inline daredevil'> [Daredevil] is a common pick, played as <img class='inline power'> Power on Slothasor and <img class='inline condition'> Condition on Matthias.

DPS classes with copious amounts of crowd control will also be extremely beneficial.

---

## Slothasor

Sloth is run with a strategy that differs significantly from the standard PUG strat, focusing on three important aspects:
1. Keeping the boss stationary as much as possible.
2. Minimising the time spent eating  <img class='inline shroom'> [Poison Mushrooms] and transformed.
3. Killing slublings quickly so that they do not disrupt the group.

The encounter can be [solo-healed](../general.html#solo-healing) if the off-sub has enough support, with classes that provide good passive healing and barrier such as <img class='inline luminary'> [Luminary] and <img class='inline ritualist'> [Ritualist].

Fast Slothasor clears are extremely dependant on the group's defiance damage: classes with heavy breakbar skills will be extremely beneficial, such as <img class='inline amalgam'> [Amalgam] with <img class='inline bombkit'> [Bomb Kit].


### Movement and Shrooms

Select one player to eat the first shroom (this is best done by the DPS class with the lowest damage). Everyone else should <img class='inline stealth'> [Stealth] (normally done with <img class='inline mass-invis'> [Mass Invisibility]) and run to the boss. The shroom player does a brief countdown and then eats the shroom to start the fight.

<img class='center bordered' width='60%' src='sloth/sloth_1.webp'>

{: .note}
Players that are affected by <img class='inline stealth'> [Stealth] will not take damage from the mushroom's posion fields.

The shroom player should eat the <img class='inline shroom'> [Poison Mushroom] next to Slothasor, taking care to stay far so that they are not bursted down by their allies. After eating, they should move to the safe spot shown in the image below. The rest of the stack should then move into the cleared area when the boss casts [Tantrum](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Tantrum).

<img class='center bordered' width='60%' src='sloth/sloth_2.webp'>

Soon after, [Slublings] will start spawning. Players should use <img class='inline pull'> [Pull] skills to group them together and bring them to the boss where they are then cleaved down. The first pull is almost always done with <img class='inline curtain'> [Temporal Curtain] due to its large area of effect.

<img class='center bordered' width='60%' src='sloth/sloth_3.webp'>

Once the slublings have been pulled to the stack, it's safe for the shroom player to go in and eat the second shroom, then return to the stack. If the group is low on DPS, they can delay returning slightly.

<img class='center bordered' width='60%' src='sloth/sloth_4.webp'>

Use the space you have available to stand in. If both shrooms respawn, you can outheal the incoming damage for a period before it becomes an issue.

### Managing the Shake

Slothasor's [Spore Release](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Spore_Release) (a.k.a. shake) is one of the more dangerous skills in the encounter. Usually it is double dodged, but any player with access to some form of <img class='inline invuln'> [Invulnerability] can block most of the shots by standing in the center of the boss's hitbox while invulnerable and jumping repeatedly. This is commonly done using <img class='inline distortion'> [Distortion].

### Skipping the final breakbar

Sloth's final breakbar occurs at 10%. With good timing, players can wait for the boss to begin an animation, then burst down the final 10% in one go, skipping the breakbar entirely. This can save as much as 10 seconds.
