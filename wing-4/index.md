Bastion of the Penitent is one of the more difficult wings to clear. Most of this wing consists of boss fights, meaning that high DPS is essential to getting a good time. Samarog and Deimos can be especially punishing, as they contain a lot of dangerous mechanics that can be run-ending if misplayed. A good composition, smooth transitions and a lot of practice are the keys to getting the achievement for this wing.

---

#### Main Points
{: .no_toc}

- High damage is especially important since most of the wings is boss fights. Every boss in this wing should not require more than one celestial stack heal.
- [Cairn] and [Muursat Overseer] are golems: do as much damage as possible.
- [Samarog] has many dangerous mechanics, and is one of the fights where groups lose the most time. Optimized positioning and split phases are extremely important.
- [Deimos] is the run-ender. Your clear is conditional on correct management of his mechanics.

---

## Composition

Wing 4 leaves you with relative liberty to make your own composition. The only essential components are:
- A <img class='inline thief'> [Thief] to open the door after [Samarog].
- A <img class='inline mesmer'> [Mesmer] to optimize the transition from [Cairn] to [Mursaat Overseer]
